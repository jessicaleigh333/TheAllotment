SIDEWALK AND PLANT CATALOG UPDATE

Extract this ZIP into a NEW folder. Keep your existing game folder as a backup.
In Godot Project Manager choose Import and select project.godot in the new folder.
Run the project (F5), then enter the garden as usual.

Raised garden entrance paths now have invisible collision ramps along both sides.
No character appearance, garden layout, grass or path visuals were replaced.

Click Flowers & shrubs - 3D catalog near the bottom right while exploring.
Choose from six flower palettes and three shrub configurations. Right-drag to
orbit and use the wheel to zoom. Back to garden returns to your current position.
These are previews of the existing procedural prototype models, not final
botanical species models or a planting/inventory system. Art review is pending.

Added: scenes/plant_catalog.tscn, scenes/plant_prototype.tscn,
scenes/curb_ramp.tscn and their scripts. Changed scripts/courtyard_world.gd.
Added tests/verify_sidewalk.gd. Original uploaded ZIPs remain unchanged.

Checks: Godot 4.6.3 headless import and runtime; nine physical entrance and exit
crossings and catalog opening. Project targets Godot 4.7; please preview in your
4.7.2 editor before treating the visuals as approved.

Existing verify_avatar.gd also passed: customization, animation, movement, UI
pause, overview, all nine garden entrances and blocking hedge collisions.
