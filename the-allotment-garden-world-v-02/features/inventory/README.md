# Backpack

Inventory now opens the shared market UI in Backpack mode. It shows available owned stacks, item details, placement/equip actions and up to six pinned common items. Free additions, counts, pins and placed items persist per local profile. Direct decoration can create repeated copies without stock; previous bag copies are used first. Browse placed items in the details column to return a placed object to the bag, including when all stacks have been placed. Profile saves are local only. Placed items may belong to the inner circle or Garden 01; their area is preserved when returning or reloading.

The Botanical catalog tab browses seven starter plant types at seed, seedling, young and mature stages, with care notes, metre dimensions and actual stage previews. Growth follows real elapsed days, including offline time; see ../market/GROWTH.md. Backpack stacks preserve stage and accumulated age when retrieving and replanting. Browse placed items displays the current stage and height of growing plants. Harvests, dormancy, seasonal dieback, garden-plan lists, sales, storage chests, online trading and a full avatar shop remain future work.

Implementation and validation: ../shop/README.md. Plants and furniture are simple reusable prototype meshes rather than the final art illustrated on item cards. User visual review is pending.
