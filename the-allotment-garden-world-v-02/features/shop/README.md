# Garden market

The Shop side button opens the full-screen French garden market. It uses new market art and an illustrated item atlas inspired by the owner's supplied reference images. The interface is composed of live Godot controls, not a flattened screenshot. Artwork is ready for visual review.

Browse All, Featured, Plants, Decor, Avatar, Tools, Seeds or Pinned. Search common or botanical names; Owned only includes items in the bag or placed in the garden. The Market, Backpack and Botanical catalog tabs share a collection. Details include a picture, care notes for plants, price, bag count and actions. Inspect 3D model opens an orbiting prototype mesh preview.

The starter collection has 16 entries: six mature plant clumps, six decor items, a flower-trimmed hat, a decorative watering can and two unavailable seed previews. Six plants, six decor items and the watering can can be placed; the hat equips the existing avatar option. Seed growing is not implemented, so seed purchases are disabled and never spend Runes. Item illustrations are richer than the deliberately simple 3D prototype models, which are labeled accordingly. No production-quality mesh integration is claimed.

Shop items are currently free, as requested. Existing Rune balances are preserved but ignored. Add to backpack adds a free copy; available placeable items can also be used directly without stock. The common-item row holds up to six pinned items. Unavailable seeds and unreadable/write-failed saves remain protected. Basic existing gardening tools are unchanged.

Choose **Decorate inner circle** directly from an item to decorate the shared paved courtyard, raised approach-path edges or the surrounding grass. No purchase or bag stock is required, and placement stays active for repeated free copies until Done/Escape. **Place in Garden 01** preserves the previous private-demo placement option. R rotates; WASD pans; wheel zooms. A green ghost indicates valid space and red indicates blocked space.

The circle adapter samples the actual paving, joint, raised approach and grass heights. Full footprints must fit the surface without straddling the curb. The center planting, nine existing planters, a 1.3-metre walking loop and nine clear entrance routes are protected. Broad stone areas and approach edges remain decoratable. Purchased-object overlap and outer bounds are also checked. Saved items carry an area tag; legacy records without one still load in Garden 01. Both areas restore on startup. The Backpack can return placed objects to the bag; free replacements remain unlimited. This is local decorating, not a shared multiplayer permission system.

Storage: user://profiles/<id>/market.json for a selected local profile; direct scene/legacy runs use user://market.json. No player save data is committed. Files include balance, bag counts, six favorite slots and placements. A malformed save is preserved and purchasing is blocked. Bounded counts and schema checks guard load; temporary-file replacement commits the complete state.

Preview: F5, enter a local profile, then Shop. Choose a Rose bush and Decorate inner circle. Repeat placements freely, or add items to Backpack. For a standalone visual scene use features/shop/screen.tscn; placement is disabled without a world.

Validation: tests/verify_courtyard_decor.gd verifies path and grass heights, repeat placement without stock, route/overlap protection, area persistence, legacy records and direct Shop entry. tests/verify_market.gd uses a fresh isolated XDG_DATA_HOME and covers free additions, persistence, profile isolation, unavailable items, corrupt-save preservation, filters, Latin-name search, 3D previews, placement constraints, consumption/reload/retrieval and HUD wiring. ui/verify_hud.gd covers all 12 destinations and Escape. Rendered Market, Backpack and Botanical catalog at 1280×720 with Godot 4.6.3 OpenGL Compatibility. The project targets 4.7; owner visual review remains pending.
